Tradexpress Community Code of Conduct
Overview and Purpose
Millions of developers across the world host millions of projects—both open and closed source—on Tradexpress. We're fortunate to be able to play a part in enabling collaboration across the developer community every day, which is a responsibility we don’t take lightly. Together, we all have the exciting opportunity to make this a community we can be proud of.   

Tradexpress Community is intended to be a place for further collaboration, support, and brainstorming. This is a civilized place for connecting with other users, learning new skills, sharing feedback and ideas, and finding all the support you need for your Tradexpress projects. By participating in Tradexpress Community, you are agreeing to the same Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policies that apply to Tradexpress, as well as this Tradexpress Community-specific Code of Conduct.   

With this Code of Conduct, we hope to help you understand how best to collaborate in Tradexpress Community, what you can expect from moderators, and what type of actions or content may result in temporary or permanent suspension from community participation. We will investigate any abuse reports and may moderate public content within Tradexpress Community that we determine to be in violation of either the Tradexpress Terms of Service or this Code of Conduct.   

Our diverse user base brings different perspectives, ideas, and experiences, and ranges from people who created their first "Hello World" project last week to the most well-known software developers in the world. We are committed to making Tradexpress an environment that welcomes all the different voices and perspectives our community has to offer, while maintaining a safe place for developers to do their best work.   

Pledge
In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to making participation in Tradexpress Community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Standards
Treat Tradexpress Community with respect. We are a shared resource — a place to share skills, knowledge, and interests through ongoing conversation.

Best Practices for Maintaining a Strong Community
The primary purpose of the Tradexpress community is to collaborate on software projects. We are committed to maintaining a community where users are free to express themselves and challenge one another's ideas, both technical and otherwise. At the same time, it's important that users remain respectful and allow space for others to contribute openly.   

Engage with consideration and respect.

Be welcoming and open-minded — New users join our community each day. Make room for opinions other than your own and be welcoming to new collaborators and those just getting started.

Be respectful — Working in a collaborative environment means disagreements may happen. Remember to criticize ideas, not people. Share thoughtful, constructive criticism and be courteous.

Be empathetic — Tradexpress is a global community with people from a wide variety of backgrounds and perspectives. Try to put yourself in others’ shoes.

Contribute in a positive and constructive way.

Improve the discussion. Always work to improve the discussion in some way, however small.

Be clear and stay on topic. Tradexpress Community is for collaboration, sharing ideas, and helping each other get stuff done. Off-topic comments are a distraction.

Share mindfully. When asking others to give you feedback or collaborate, only share valuable and relevant resources. Avoid sharing sensitive information like your own email address.

Keep it tidy. Put things in the right place, don't cross-post the same thing in multiple discussions, and use upvote buttons rather than low-value replies.

Be trustworthy.

Always be honest. Don’t knowingly share incorrect information or intentionally mislead other Tradexpress Community participants.

What is not Allowed
Anyone under the age of 13. Accounts belonging to children under the age of 13 will be closed.

Creating new accounts after restriction. Maintaining more than one free account or creating new accounts to bypass restrictions is prohibited.

Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Violation of Terms of Service.

Reasonable use of AI generated content
We love experimenting with new technologies, but as with all new technology, guidelines must be followed when using generative AI tools:

Take personal responsibility for everything you post.   

Read and revise the content before you post it; use your own authentic voice.   

Use your expertise as a developer to verify that the answer works and makes sense.   

Do not just post AI-generated content verbatim to inflate your reputation.   

Be careful not to mislead other users into thinking they are receiving an official response from Tradexpress staff.

Enforcement
What Tradexpress Community Participants Can Do
If you see a problem, report it. When you see bad behavior, don’t reply. Report the disruptive user or disruptive content to Tradexpress.

Our Responsibilities
Actions we may take in response to a flag or abuse report include, but are not limited to:

Content Removal

Content Blocking

Tradexpress Account Suspension

Tradexpress Account Termination

Contacting Tradexpress Staff
If you want to contact Tradexpress Staff, Community Managers, Administrators, or Moderators privately, please use our designated Support contact form. Unsolicited mentions, pings, or out-of-channel outreach are strongly discouraged.

Legal Notices
To use this service, you must agree to abide by our Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policies, and Privacy Statement. Tradexpress retains full discretion to remove or restrict any content or accounts for policy violations.

Data Retention and Deletion of Data
You may access, update, alter, or delete your basic user profile information by editing your user profile or contacting Tradexpress Support. Barring legal requirements, we will delete your full profile (within reason) within 90 days of your request.
